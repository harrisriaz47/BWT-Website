THE CHALLENGE

Why Data Modernization Projects Fail

Most platforms stall between proof-of-concept and production-ready. We've seen the patterns.

Pattern 1

Tactical Execution Without Strategy

Teams rush to implement tools (Snowflake, Databricks) without aligning to business outcomes or total cost modeling. Result: fragmented estates and spiraling cloud bills.

Our Approach: Every engagement starts with diagnosis—business context, constraints, success criteria—before architecture decisions.

Pattern 2

AI Pilot Purgatory

Models live in notebooks. No CI/CD, no observability, no compliance path. 80% of AI projects never reach production.

Our Approach: Production-first MLOps with automated testing, drift detection, and governance baked in from day one.

Pattern 3

Vendor Lock-In by Accident

Proprietary SDKs and platform-specific code make switching vendors costly. Future optionality disappears.

Our Approach: Vendor-neutral design patterns. Infrastructure-as-code. Portability validated before handoff.
