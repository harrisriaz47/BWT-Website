Yes. Our delivery patterns include access controls, audit evidence generation, and documentation that maps to common compliance needs.

What does “inspectable deliverables” mean?

You receive structured documentation, decision logs, runbooks, and operational dashboards—so you can review quality and maintainability before scaling investment.

How do you measure success?

We align to business KPIs and operational metrics (freshness, reliability, latency, cost) with an explicit cadence for reviewing progress and outcomes.

Do you help after go-live?

Yes. We can provide ongoing DataOps/MLOps support, SLO reviews, and iterative improvements based on production learning.
