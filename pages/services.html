DECISION GUIDE

Choosing the Right Engagement

Do you have a clear technical roadmap?

No → Start with Strategic Advisory to define strategy, build business cases, and create implementation roadmap.
Yes → Move to platform engineering or managed operations.

Do you have internal engineering capacity?

Limited or none → Choose Platform Engineering with embedded teams or turnkey delivery.
Yes, but need expertise → Consider Advisory for architecture reviews and guidance.

Do you need ongoing 24/7 operations?

Yes → Managed Operations provides SLA-backed reliability and continuous optimization.
No → Platform Engineering projects include transition/handoff with training.
