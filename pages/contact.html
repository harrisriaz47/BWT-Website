COMMON QUESTIONS

Before You Reach Out

What size companies do you work with?

Our sweet spot: $50M-$500M revenue companies with 5-50 person data teams. We also advise Series B+ startups building data-first products.

What's your typical engagement length?

Advisory: 2-4 weeks. Platform builds: 3-6 months. Managed services: ongoing with quarterly reviews.

Do you staff augment or build complete teams?

Neither. We embed small, specialized teams (2-4 people) to deliver specific outcomes, then hand off to your internal team. Think of us as a strike team, not contractors.

What if we're already working with a systems integrator?

We often complement SIs. They handle enterprise rollout, we handle deep technical design and prototyping. We can also audit SI work.

How do you price engagements?

Fixed-scope with milestone-based payments. Advisory: $15K-$50K. Platform builds: $150K-$500K. We provide estimates after scoping call.
