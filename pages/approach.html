Delivery Framework

Transparent, iterative, instrumentation-driven execution.

01

Discovery & Alignment

Landscape mapping, dependency analysis, risk modeling, and goal formalization. Output: architecture draft + measurable success metrics.

02

Design & Validation

Data models, interface contracts, POCs or prototypes where uncertainty is high. Early observability scaffolds established.

03

Incremental Build

Two-week delivery cycles with integrated QA, code review, performance profiling, and model drift checks.

04

Launch & Scale

Hardening, failover rehearsals, capacity tuning, continuous optimization, and enablement for in-house teams.
