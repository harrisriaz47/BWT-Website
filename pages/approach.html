METHODOLOGY

Four-Phase Delivery Model

Every engagement follows a structured, transparent process with decision gates and executive-ready artifacts.

PHASE 1 · DIAGNOSE

Current State Assessment

We inventory your data landscape: sources, pipelines, warehouses, BI tools, team capabilities. Identify technical debt, performance bottlenecks, security gaps, and cost inefficiencies.

  • • Architecture baseline documentation
  • • Stakeholder interviews (business + technical)
  • • Constraint mapping (budget, timeline, compliance)
  • • Threat model and risk register
Success Criteria Definition

Collaborate with your team to define measurable outcomes: query latency targets, cost reduction goals, compliance requirements, adoption KPIs.

example_success_criteria: performance: - p99 query latency < 2s - data freshness < 15min cost: - 30% reduction in warehouse spend governance: - 100% data lineage coverage

DELIVERABLES

Assessment report · Roadmap options · ROI thesis · Security review · Data handling plan

PHASE 2 · DESIGN

Reference Architecture

We design a target-state architecture with explicit trade-offs documented. Stack selection (Snowflake vs. Databricks, batch vs. streaming) grounded in your constraints, not vendor relationships.

  • • Logical + physical architecture diagrams
  • • Interface contracts and data models
  • • Security and access control design
  • • Cost projection model (TCO over 3 years)
Prototype & Validation

Build small prototypes to validate critical unknowns: can we hit the latency target? Does the proposed feature store scale? Reduce risk before full build.

validation_spikes: - Load test: 10K events/sec ingestion - Query performance: complex join latency - Cost simulation: 3-month projection - Integration test: BI tool compatibility

DELIVERABLES

Architecture pack · Stack decision log · Prototype results · Executive readout · Go/no-go criteria

PHASE 3 · DELIVER

Iterative Build

We build in two-week sprints with demos, automated testing, and integrated QA. Code lives in your repos. Backlogs live in your Jira. Full transparency, no vendor black box.

  • • Infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, CloudFormation)
  • • CI/CD pipelines with automated tests
  • • Data quality checks and lineage tracking
  • • Recurring KPI dashboards and burn-down tracking
Observability & Cost Control

Every pipeline, model, and dashboard ships with monitoring. Cost alerts, performance profiling, and data quality gates prevent surprises in production.

observability_standards: - Datadog / Prometheus for infra metrics - dbt tests for data quality (100% coverage) - Monte Carlo for lineage + anomaly detection - FinOps dashboards with budget alerts

DELIVERABLES

Production platform · IaC repos · CI/CD pipelines · Observability stack · Documentation wiki

PHASE 4 · OPERATE

Knowledge Transfer

We don't just hand over code—we ensure your team can operate it. Training sessions, runbooks, office hours, and shadowing periods build internal capability.

  • • Runbooks for common operations and incidents
  • • Video walkthroughs of critical workflows
  • • Architectural decision records (ADRs)
  • • Office hours during transition (4-6 weeks)
Managed Operations Option

If you prefer, we can operate the platform as a managed service: 24/7 monitoring, incident response, optimization, and continuous improvement.

managed_ops_slas: - 24/7 on-call + incident response - Monthly health reports + optimization reviews - Quarterly roadmap updates - Proactive cost management

DELIVERABLES

Runbooks · Training materials · ADRs · Transition plan · Optional: Managed ops contract
